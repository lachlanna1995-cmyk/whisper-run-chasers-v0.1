# STAR BASE visual rebuild

## Goal
Create a portrait-first mobile survival strategy base that has the readability and inhabited-world feel of successful mobile base builders while remaining visually original to STAR BASE.

## Non-negotiable
- Do not render buildings as colored rectangles, circles, or plain geometric prisms.
- Use actual sprite art for buildings and environment objects.
- The base should read as a place: roads, paths, fences, pipes, lights, debris, storage, workers, drones, signage, damage, and active machinery.
- Keep portrait orientation.
- Base management is the home experience. Deployments are entered from the base.
- No copied Last Z or Clash assets, icons, names, layouts, or art. Use them only as product-level references for readability and density.

## Camera and interaction
- Isometric 2.5D perspective.
- Pan by dragging.
- Pinch zoom.
- Buildings are large enough to recognize on a phone.
- Tap building to show contextual upgrade/move/info actions.
- Build tray opens from bottom.
- New structures use ghost placement and grid snapping.

## Visual language
- Industrial frontier science fiction.
- Salvaged military infrastructure mixed with advanced orbital technology.
- Dark steel, weathered concrete, oxidized alloy, illuminated cyan systems, amber hazard lighting.
- Buildings must have distinct silhouettes.
- Upgrade stages add visible size/details instead of only changing a number.

## Base life
- Workers and service drones travel between structures.
- Fans rotate, lights blink, reactors pulse, exhaust vents emit smoke/steam.
- Roads and service lanes connect important structures.
- Small props prevent empty tile fields.

## Launch interface
- Top: commander/base identity and compact resources.
- Right rail: deploy, faction, server/map.
- Bottom: build and contextual building actions.
- No storefront permanently occupying the base screen.
- Purchases remain optional and separated from progression.
